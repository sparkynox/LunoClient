Built on giants' shoulders

LunoClient is developed by SparkyNox, built on top of the MojoLauncher source code — itself a fork of PojavLauncher.

Full credit goes to the original MojoLauncher and PojavLauncher authors and contributors — this project wouldn't exist without their work. LunoClient keeps every original license and copyright notice intact.

What LunoClient adds

On top of that proven engine, LunoClient brings a full anime-themed interface, a guided first-run experience, visual polish across every screen, and an ongoing focus on making the app feel light on lower-end devices.

It's early — this is a beta. Expect fast iteration, and feel free to follow progress or report issues on GitHub.
